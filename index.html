About DJ Huevo

DJ Huevo is a San Antonio-based DJ and recording artist building his own sound at the intersection of Latin street energy and cinematic production. Full gear. Full command of the room. Every set is an experience.

His debut album After Party Privado drops June 2, 2026 — a project built in San Antonio, made for everyone. Featuring tracks including Tu Padre, Mueve Ese Culo, INCENTIVO, and more.

Available for clubs, private events, festivals, and brand activations across Texas and beyond. $100/hr minimum. Full production setup always included.
